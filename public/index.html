How to survive

Controls

  • WASD / arrows - move
  • Mouse - aim. Hold the button (or Space) to hit, eat, shoot or build
  • 1-8 / wheel - select an item, click a slot to select it
  • Drag slots to reorder, drag out or Q to drop
  • E - add wood to a campfire / use a tent
  • Enter - chat   H - this help   M - sound
  • Drag on the left side of the screen - move
  • ACT - hold to hit, eat, shoot or build
  • Tap the world - hit, shoot or build right there
  • Tap a slot to take an item, a recipe to craft it
  • Drag slots to reorder, drag one out to drop it
  • E - at a campfire or a tent   … - chat

Tips

  • Punch trees and rocks, then craft an axe and a pickaxe.
  • Eat berries or cooked meat before you starve.
  • Nights are cold and dark. A campfire keeps you warm - feed it wood.
  • Wolves hunt at night but fear fire. Dire wolves do not...
  • Build walls, spikes and a tent to respawn at your camp.
